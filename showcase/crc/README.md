# showcase/crc/

The home of a planned showcase with Climate Risk Commons (CRC): a parametric insurance study on CRC and VELO/CDT data, through the CRC adapter in `packages/crc`. It's an exploratory collaboration, and its scope isn't decided yet. This folder holds this README, the licence, the [collaboration scan](COLLABORATION-SCAN.md) of CRC's public repositories (options and questions) and a [meeting brief](MEETING-BRIEF.md).

- No CRC or VELO data is committed. Data terms are recorded in [`docs/data-sources.md`](../../docs/data-sources.md).
- Notebooks hold no library logic. That lives in `packages/core` and `packages/crc`.
- Licence: the showcase's own code is BSD-3-Clause ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). The AGPL applies to the combined work with the CRC adapter ([ADR 0001](../../docs/adr/0001-name-and-licence.md)).

The repository's [README](../../README.md) has the project's status.
